# Licensing and edition policy

This repository currently uses a source-available license. The public edition provides the evidence contract, local validation, privacy boundary, request hashing, response verification, synthetic fixtures, and release provenance. The private decision Engine remains separately hosted and controlled.

## Public edition

Use the Gateway within the rights granted by `LICENSE`. The public package does not grant access to a private endpoint, private prompts, weights, thresholds, customer data, credentials, or commercial support.

## Reserved material

The following remain private or separately licensed:

- the decision Engine and its prompts, weights, thresholds, formulas, and deployment configuration;
- customer evidence bundles, supplier records, production requests, and production responses;
- managed decision services, custom integrations, and enterprise support.

## Official release status

Only releases whose manifest and publisher provenance signature verify against the published key are official PDBen-Auto releases. This document explains the edition boundary; it does not grant rights beyond `LICENSE` and is not legal advice.
